Who We Are

Welcome to University Hostel

A premium student residence built for comfort, safety, and academic focus.

Trusted by 100+ Students

Quality Student Living in Peshawar

University Hostel for Boys is located at Danish Abad, Nimat Mahal, Railway Society, Near Masjid Nimra, Peshawar. We provide a peaceful, clean, and fully equipped environment ideal for students focused on their studies.

We believe every student deserves a safe and comfortable place to live. Our hostel is managed by dedicated staff who ensure everything runs smoothly 24/7.

  • Prime location near universities
  • Safe & secure environment
  • Dedicated study spaces
  • Friendly & supportive management
